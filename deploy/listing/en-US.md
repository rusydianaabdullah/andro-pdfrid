## Full description (4000 chars max)

Play accepts a small amount of formatting in the full description: `<b>`, `<i>`, `<u>`, `<br>`
and simple lists. The version below uses plain paragraphs and bullet-style dashes, which
survive every renderer.

```
Pdfrid opens, reads and marks up PDF files entirely on your phone. There is no account, no
ads, and no internet connection at any point — the app does not request the INTERNET
permission at all, and in fact requests no permissions whatsoever. Documents arrive as files
you pick or share in, and nothing about them ever leaves the device.

<b>Reading</b>
Continuous vertical scrolling through the whole document, pages rendered on demand and cached
so a 500-page file opens as fast as a one-pager. Pinch to zoom up to 6x with panning, or
double-tap for a quick 2.5x. Rotate in 90° steps, invert to a night mode for reading in the
dark, and jump around with a thumbnail grid or the page scrollbar on the right edge.

<b>Search, select, copy</b>
Full-text search highlights every match and steps through them one at a time. Long-press a
word to select it, drag either grip to adjust the selection, then copy it, search for it
elsewhere in the document, or hand it to another app — or grab an entire page's text in one
tap from the menu.

<b>Annotate</b>
Highlight, underline and strikethrough by dragging over text; a freehand brush for anything
else; pinned notes and typed text you can drop anywhere and drag to reposition afterwards. Six
ink colours, undo, and a list of every mark in the document that jumps straight to it.
Annotations are kept alongside the app's own data rather than written into the file you
opened — "Export annotated copy" writes a flattened copy with the marks burned in and hands it
to the share sheet, so the original is never modified.

<b>Split and merge</b>
Cut a PDF into separate files by page range (1-3, 5, 8-10, or one file per page), or join
several PDFs into one in whatever order you arrange them. Both go through a real PDF library
that copies pages rather than re-rendering them as images, so text stays selectable, fonts
stay embedded, and file sizes stay small.

<b>Password-protected files</b>
Open them by entering the password once.

<b>Opens from anywhere</b>
Registers for "Open with" and "Share to" on any application/pdf file, so it shows up from a
files app, a browser, an email client, or another app's share sheet.

<b>Permissions, and why</b>
None. Not one. Documents are opened as content URIs the system hands over when you pick or
share a file; nothing is read from storage directly, and nothing is sent anywhere, because the
app has no way to send anything anywhere.

<b>Languages</b>
English and Bahasa Indonesia, following your system setting.
```
