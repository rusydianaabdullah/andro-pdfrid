Quarterly Field Report

Northwind Research Cooperative · Third Quarter, 2026

1. Overview

This report summarizes site activity, instrument calibration, and preliminary findings collected between July and September. Fieldwork proceeded on schedule at all four monitoring stations, with only Station C losing two days to weather. Data completeness across the quarter stands at 97.4 percent, above the 95 percent threshold set in the project charter.

Three new sensors were commissioned at Station A in early August, extending coverage of the western basin for the first time since the program began. Early readings are consistent with the modeled baseline, and a full calibration pass is scheduled for the next quarter.

3. Preliminary Findings

Aggregate readings continue the gradual upward trend first noted in Q1, though the rate of change has slowed relative to the previous two quarters. The working hypothesis remains consistent with the seasonal model described in Appendix B, and nothing in this quarter's data requires revising it.

Two anomalous readings were logged at Station B on August 22 and August 23. Both were traced to a loose sensor mount rather than an environmental signal, and the affected samples have been excluded from the aggregate figures above.

  • Baseline drift remains within the ±0.3 unit tolerance across all four stations.
  • Cross-station correlation held above 0.91 for the full quarter.
  • No sensor required replacement outside of routine rotation.

The team recommends continuing the current sampling cadence into Q4 without modification, and revisiting the calibration schedule only if the new Station A sensors show drift outside tolerance during their first full quarter of operation.

4. Next Quarter

Planned work for the fourth quarter includes the full calibration pass at Station A, a firmware update to the logging units at Stations B and D, and the annual mount inspection at all four sites ahead of the winter weather window.

A revised budget for the calibration pass has been submitted separately and is pending approval from the steering committee. Field staff have been notified of the tentative schedule and no conflicts have been raised to date.
